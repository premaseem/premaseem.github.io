About Aseem

Engineer by profession.
Teacher by heart.

Prem Aseem Jain is an Austin-based architect who has spent two decades building software that serves hundreds of millions of people, and a teacher who helps others grow, in code and in breath.

01

The architect

Aseem is a Data & AI architect, cloud architect and tech lead with deep expertise in identity and IAM security. At a leading global gaming company, he led the identity platform behind 300 million accounts and delivered integrations with Netflix Games, Amazon Luna, PlayStation, NBA and Apple.

His recent work is AI in production: an AI moderation service handling 2M requests a day, RAG systems, multi-agent workflows and custom MCP tools.

The teacher

Aseem is the author of the Packt course Learn Design Patterns with Java. His blog has 8.5M views, and his YouTube tutorials have been watched for more than 43,000 hours. He mentors engineers on their way to becoming architects and leads.

03

The practitioner

Beyond technology, Aseem teaches breathwork, yoga and meditation and volunteers in his community. He is a pilot and cyclist with a life full of colour and curiosity.
